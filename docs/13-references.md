## Link verification

`scripts/check_links.py` requested all 91 URLs in this repository on 18 November 2026:

- **61 returned HTTP 200 directly.**
- **30 returned 401/403/429** because the publisher blocks automated clients (Garden of Life, Elsevier, Wiley, Oxford Academic, MDPI, BMJ, Nestlé, Top Class Actions).
- **0 returned 404.**

A bot block is not a dead link, but it is also not verification. Every one of the 30 was therefore re-checked through Crossref, PubMed, Semantic Scholar, the Wayback Machine or a rendered copy, and the outcome for each is recorded in [14 — Source verification log](14-source-verification.md). Four citations turned out to be wrong or unverifiable: one had the wrong journal, two pointed at the wrong page, one secondary news link could not be read anywhere and was removed.

## Notes on source quality

- **Consumer Reports** and **Clean Label Project** are advocacy-adjacent but use accredited labs and published methods; their results are the backbone of the contamination section.
- **Labdoor** publishes a pass/fail screen but keeps per-metal numbers behind a paywall; only the outcome is cited.
- **Mamavation** is an advocacy site; its methods are unusually detailed (EPA 1633, 587 pesticides, ICP-MS) and its Garden of Life result is therefore cited with the caveat that it is a single sample of one flavor.
- **NaturalNews' 2014 claims** of extreme tungsten, lead and cadmium in Garden of Life RAW Protein were reviewed and are **not** relied on in this report. The outlet is not a credible analytical source, the raw data were never independently replicated, and the claims were entangled with a commercial campaign. The 2015 Prop 65 settlement is the reliable evidence that lead and cadmium were a documented problem.
- **Garden of Life's public statements** are quoted as claims, not evidence.
